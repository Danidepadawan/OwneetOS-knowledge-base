# Optional apps

- The user installs **only the apps they want**: at installation and later, from the apps page,
  where installed and missing apps are shown differently ([[step-5.0-optional-apps]]).
- When step 5.0 arrives **the ISO becomes slim**: apps are downloaded when chosen (option b),
  instead of shipping in the image ([[distribution-and-updates]]).
- In the live system, apps follow the same RAM rule as Steam ([[steam-integration]]).
