# Guards

Things that must **never** be done. They are loaded in every session and re-injected word for word
after a compaction. A guard can only be added, changed or removed with the owner's agreement
([[kb-rules]]). When a task seems to need breaking one: stop and ask the owner.

- [[collaboration]] — working with the owner: approvals, asking, recording decisions.
- [[host-safety]] — the developer's PC and the development environment.
- [[secrets-and-signing]] — signing keys, credentials, what may be distributed unsigned.
- [[product-invariants]] — what OwneetOS must never become.
- [[legal]] — licences, trademarks, redistribution, privacy.
- [[user-data]] — the user's disks, games and data.
