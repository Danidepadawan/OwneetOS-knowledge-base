# Guard: legal

- **Never redistribute** Widevine, the Xbox wireless dongle firmware, Steam's runtime, games or
  store content: the user's own system downloads them, with consent where required.
  ([[legal-compliance]])
- **Never let CI publish downloadable ISOs or packages** until every release ships its GPL source
  archive (roadmap step 7.2). ([[legal-compliance]])
- **Never ship assets under NonCommercial or NoDerivatives terms** (e.g. CC BY-NC-SA); only free
  licences that allow commercial use and redistribution. ([[legal-compliance]])
- **Never use "Pegasus" or Pegasus's logos** as the frontend's name or logo, never remove Pegasus's
  copyright notices, never ship its CC BY-NC-SA theme. ([[frontend-fork]])
- **Never bundle third-party logos** (Netflix, Spotify, Xbox, Arch…) in the repository, the ISO or
  the UI; never imply endorsement. Third-party names only say what works with OwneetOS.
  ([[legal-compliance]])
- **Never use modded clients, self-bots, DRM circumvention, scraping or undocumented store APIs**:
  only official clients and web apps, local data first. ([[legal-compliance]], [[online-and-chat]])
- **Never add a third-party component without crediting it** in `CREDITS.md` in the same change.
  ([[legal-compliance]])
- **Never collect personal data** without explicit consent and a GDPR-compliant privacy notice.
  ([[legal-compliance]])
- **Never install a game from its installer** without first showing "By installing you accept the
  game's licence" [Read] [Install]. ([[games-on-disk]])
